## 🌟 Overview

The programme began with everyday AI and progressed through **learning from sources**, **creative video production** and **building a working application**. This final module brings those ideas together through a beginner-friendly knowledge challenge, an invitation to reflect on the learning experience and practical suggestions for continued exploration.

**The goal is not memorising tool menus.** It is understanding what AI can help with, how to evaluate its output and how to apply it responsibly.

## 🎯 Learning objectives

After completing this module, you should be able to:

- Summarise the practical difference between **discovering**, **learning with**, **creating with** and **building with** AI.
- Answer foundational questions about prompting, source verification, media generation and app testing.
- Identify one AI application relevant to your own studies, work or everyday life.
- Give constructive feedback on the learning experience.
- Choose a safe, achievable next project to practise independently.

## 🧠 AI Knowledge Challenge — Eight MCQs

Choose your answer before expanding **Answer & Explanation**. This is a self-check exercise, not a formal or graded examination. The explanations can be revealed on GitHub by expanding the corresponding section.

### 🔴 Q1. Which is a familiar example of AI in everyday life?

A. A USB drive storing a document <br>
B. Video recommendations based on viewing interests <br>
C. A printer printing a PDF <br>
D. A keyboard typing a message <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: B. Video recommendations based on viewing interests**

**Explanation:** Recommendation systems can analyse viewing behaviour and content patterns to suggest relevant videos. The other activities do not inherently require AI.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q2. Which instruction is most likely to produce a useful AI response?

A. Explain it. <br>
B. Write about computers, and make it nice. <br>
C. Explain computer memory to a beginner with an example and three key points. <br>
D. Give me everything you know. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: C. Explain computer memory to a beginner with an example and three key points.**

**Explanation:** A clear task, audience, context and expected format help an AI assistant deliver a more relevant answer. Verification is still important.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q3. What makes Gemini Notebook particularly useful for source-based learning?

A. It uses supplied sources and links answers to supporting passages. <br>
B. It guarantees every uploaded document is accurate. <br>
C. It replaces the need to read original sources. <br>
D. It checks every claim against the entire internet automatically. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: A. It uses supplied sources and links answers to supporting passages.**

**Explanation:** Source-grounded Q&A lets learners trace answers to uploaded material. Citations help with checking but do not guarantee that the source itself is correct.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q4. Why should we verify important answers generated by AI?

A. AI never provides factual information. <br>
B. AI responses are always verified automatically. <br>
C. A confident tone proves a statement is correct. <br>
D. An AI response can sound convincing while containing errors. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: D. An AI response can sound convincing while containing errors.**

**Explanation:** Generative AI can produce plausible but inaccurate claims. Check important facts against trustworthy primary or authoritative sources.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q5. In AI video production, what does image-to-video mean?

A. Animating a reference image into moving footage <br>
B. Exporting one still frame from a movie <br>
C. Shrinking an image file <br>
D. Converting a photo to a text document <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: A. Animating a reference image into moving footage**

**Explanation:** Image-to-video generation uses a still image plus motion instructions to produce moving scenes, often with camera, subject and environmental motion.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q6. In a product advertisement, what is the main role of voiceover compared with background music?

A. Voiceover controls camera motion; music controls focus. <br>
B. They both replace the need for visual storytelling. <br>
C. Voiceover communicates spoken information; music supports the mood. <br>
D. Background music automatically verifies product claims. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: C. Voiceover communicates spoken information; music supports the mood.**

**Explanation:** Narration delivers the spoken message, while music shapes atmosphere and emotional pacing. They should be mixed so speech stays understandable.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q7. What should you do after an AI tool generates a task-planning app?

A. Share it immediately without using it. <br>
B. Test key interactions, calculations and data handling. <br>
C. Remove input validation to make it simpler. <br>
D. Avoid requesting improvements to the design. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: B. Test key interactions, calculations and data handling.**

**Explanation:** Generated software must be tested. Create, edit and complete tasks, check progress calculations, and verify data storage and input validation.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q8. Which choice best demonstrates responsible AI use?

A. Sharing passwords to get more personalised answers <br>
B. Publishing generated claims without checking them <br>
C. Uploading confidential information to every AI tool <br>
D. Verifying outputs and protecting private or sensitive information <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: D. Verifying outputs and protecting private or sensitive information**

**Explanation:** Responsible use means checking important claims, maintaining privacy, respecting permissions and applying human judgement.

</details>

## 🌱 What can you do next?

Choose **one small, achievable project** and improve it through practice:

- **Learn:** Upload a publicly shareable document to Gemini Notebook and turn it into a useful study guide, mind map or quiz.
- **Create:** Produce a short cinematic scene showing a real action, then experiment with voice and music.
- **Build:** Adapt My Smart Planner to a different need, test every function and document what changed.
- **Verify:** Compare an AI-generated factual response with at least one reliable primary source.

A useful habit is to keep your original prompt, updated prompts, best result and a note of what you verified. That makes the learning process repeatable.

## 🛡️ Four principles to remember

1. **Think first:** Define the problem and decide what success means.
2. **Guide clearly:** Give relevant context, constraints and desired output formats.
3. **Verify and test:** Check facts, media claims and software behaviour.
4. **Respect people and data:** Protect privacy, obtain permission and credit sources appropriately.
